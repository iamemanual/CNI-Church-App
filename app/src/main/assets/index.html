Vision & Purpose

Crafted as a prayerful, sacred digital companion to serve the worshipping community of All Saints' Church. Designed to unite scripture, the liturgical almanac calendar, hymns, community announcements, and pastoral tools in one intuitive, fluid experience.

✨ 120Hz Fluid Physics
Subpixel GPU dock gestures & rubber-band feedback.
📖 Liturgical Engine
Daily Lectionary, Hindi & English hymnody, PDF reader.
⚡ Real-Time Sync
RFC 6455 WebSockets & Supabase cloud synchronization.
🕊️ OmniRoute AI
Integrated local AI gateway for scripture & ministry queries.
